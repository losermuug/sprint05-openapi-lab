UE-5 training mock. No production Corg.ly API is claimed. Photo and audio fixtures validate multipart transport only. Photos are not stored; bark meaning is deterministic; webhook deliveries are disabled. Bearer token corgly-lab-einstein-2026 is a public opaque demo token, not a JWT.
